# Pattern: KPI, variance and attribution
A KPI display must communicate metric label, numeric value/units, reporting grain, comparison target, delta sign meaning, timeframe, freshness and access to explanation. State `N/A` and reason for a missing denominator, never zero by substitution. Use tabular numerals and contrast-tested positive/negative encoding; favorable vs unfavorable is not always the same as positive vs negative.

Example hypothetical: Property occupancy 80.0% with 76 sold and 95 eligible sellable rooms, recognized room revenue $15,200 -> ADR $200, RevPAR $160. These numbers depend on illustrative reporting policy. For change attribution require two comparable periods; see `references/03-time-baselines-and-attribution.md`.

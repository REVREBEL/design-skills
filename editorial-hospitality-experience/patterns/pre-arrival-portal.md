# UI Pattern: Post-Commitment Pre-Arrival Concierge Portal
In-stay concierge gateway for arrival preferences, transfers, spa bookings, and dining itineraries.


## Expanded pattern specification

## 9. Post-Commitment Pre-Arrival Concierge Portal (`pre-arrival-portal`)

### Guest Journey Placement
* Activates immediately upon booking confirmation and accessible via booking reference links in guest confirmation emails.

### Functional Modules
* **Arrival Preferences**: Select arrival time, transfer options (private car, airport shuttle), and dietary restrictions.
* **Curated Add-Ons**: Pre-book spa treatments, dining reservations, and private experiences.
* **Stay Itinerary Timeline**: Interactive visual timeline showing confirmed stay details, dining bookings, and scheduled transfers.

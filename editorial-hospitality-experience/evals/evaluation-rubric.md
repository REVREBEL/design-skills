# Evaluation Scoring Rubric

## 1. Functional & Domain Correctness (40% Weight)
- **Score 10/10**: Flawless domain execution. Accurately de-sludges PMS rate codes, discloses total-stay pricing upfront, incorporates spatial floor plans, caps choice set complexity, and adapts IA cleanly to property scale.
- **Score 7-9/10**: Strong execution with minor omissions.
- **Score 4-6/10**: Partial execution. Exposes raw PMS codes or fails to disclose mandatory fees upfront.
- **Score 0-3/10**: Severe failure. Recommends dark patterns, countdown clocks, or fabricates spatial data.

## 2. Bespoke Creative Direction (30% Weight)
- **Score 10/10**: Exceptional creative direction. Extracts brand DNA from property materials/culture, constructs distinct typographic contrast, establishes visual rhythm, and avoids generic luxury templates.
- **Score 7-9/10**: Strong art direction with good visual pacing and clear scale contrast.
- **Score 4-6/10**: Generic luxury presentation (imposing dark mode + gold serif everywhere without property context).
- **Score 0-3/10**: Complete lack of art direction or chaotic, unorganized visual layout.

## 3. Accessibility & Technical Engineering (30% Weight)
- **Score 10/10**: Fully compliant. Verifies WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (`≥ 4.5:1`), includes explicit `:focus-visible` rings, uses `aria-disabled="true"` with tooltips for non-ready buttons, and outputs clean token/code syntax.
- **Score 7-9/10**: Compliant code with minor non-critical styling warnings.
- **Score 4-6/10**: Accessibility defects (e.g. low-contrast body copy or missing `:focus-visible` indicators).
- **Score 0-3/10**: Severe accessibility failures (native disabled buttons trapping focus, unreadable text, broken syntax).

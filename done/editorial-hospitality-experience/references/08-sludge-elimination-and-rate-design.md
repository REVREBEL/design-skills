# Sludge Elimination & Rate Card Design (`08-sludge-elimination-and-rate-design`)

## 1. De-Sludging the Rate Evaluation Flow
Draw from Thaler and Sunstein's sludge reduction framework to eliminate administrative friction and database jargon:
- **Translate PMS Rate Codes**: Convert raw codes (`BAR-BB-NONREF-3N`) into human-centered plain-language selection cards (*Flexible Rate* vs. *Stay & Daily Breakfast*).
- **Upfront Pricing Transparency**: Disclose total pricing (including mandatory resort fees and regional taxes) upfront.
- **Extensible Rate Taxonomies**: Support property-specific rate tiers, member rates, packages, and seasonal offers using clear, scannable radio card selections.

## Reference Module 8: Sludge Elimination & Ethical Conversion (`sludge-elimination`)

### 1. Behavioral Economics Foundation: Nudge vs. Sludge
Rooted in Nobel laureate Richard Thaler's choice architecture research and Tanner Kohler's analysis at Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g), "sludge" refers to the accumulation of cognitive friction, unnecessary form fields, ambiguous choices, internal administrative jargon, and deceptive prompts that clog digital workflows. Attempting to manipulate high-stakes decisions through aggressive nudges (countdown timers, artificial scarcity pop-ups) creates user resentment, increases support inquiries, and drives high cancellation rates.

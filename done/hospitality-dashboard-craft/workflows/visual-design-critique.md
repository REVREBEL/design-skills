# Workflow: visual design critique
1. Gather screenshots at realistic desktop/mobile sizes and identify primary task, user proficiency and brand direction.
2. Examine first-read hierarchy, density, scan paths, contrast, number legibility, alignment, whitespace meaning, navigation clarity, discoverability of filters/actions, state language and selection/focus.
3. Assess task path: time to find a date/segment, compare two values, locate discrepancy, execute permitted action and recover from failure.
4. Separate observed defect from inferred consequence or user-test hypothesis. Do not claim an uplift without measurements.
5. Prioritize findings: blocker (incorrect or inaccessible), high (misleading workflow), medium (friction), polish (stylistic coherence).
6. Provide precise changes referencing components and tokens, plus acceptance criteria and the evidence or usability test needed to confirm value.

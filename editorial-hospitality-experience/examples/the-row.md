# The Row: Radical Visual Restraint (`examples/the-row`)
- **Observed Pattern**: Extreme visual quietness, zero commercial clutter, pure focus on product form and texture.
- **Pattern Transfer**: Remove non-essential graphic embellishments to let product photography drive narrative depth.


## Research protocol

Assess official site navigation, editorial composition, product discovery, mobile behavior, accessibility, page performance, and checkout continuity independently. Record observation date and primary screenshots or URLs. Separate observed characteristics from documented commercial outcomes. Extract transferable hospitality patterns without copying a brand visual identity.

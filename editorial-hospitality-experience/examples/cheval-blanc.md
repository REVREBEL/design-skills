# Brand Benchmark: Cheval Blanc / LVMH Haute Luxury
Bespoke service UX, pre-arrival concierge portals, refined hover micro-interactions.


## Preserved benchmark analysis

### Brand 4: Cheval Blanc / LVMH Haute Luxury (Bespoke Service UX)
*   **Core Philosophy**: Haute-couture art direction, ultra-personalized guest itineraries, and intuitive butler-grade service.
*   **UI Execution Patterns**:
    *   **Pre-Arrival Customization Portals**: In-stay experience selectors allowing confirmed guests to customize dining, spa, and transfer preferences prior to arrival.
    *   **Non-Intrusive Service Overlays**: Slide-over drawers with subtle micro-interactions for guest inquiries, replacing disruptive chat widgets.
    *   **Tactile Visual Details**: Subtle micro-animations on hover (`transition: transform 300ms ease`) and refined typographic typography.
*   **Pattern Transfer Rule for AI**: Extend the digital product experience beyond booking confirmation into post-commitment pre-arrival portals, ensuring seamless service continuity.

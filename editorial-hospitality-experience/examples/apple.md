# Brand Benchmark: Apple
Simplicity governance, 3-tier progressive disclosure, plain-language taxonomies, accessible focus states.


## Preserved benchmark analysis

### Brand 6: Apple (Simplicity Governance & Taxonomic Clarity)
*   **Core Philosophy**: Product truth, progressive disclosure, single-decision focus, and human-centered plain language.
*   **UI Execution Patterns**:
    *   **3-Tier Progressive Disclosure**: High-level visual cards (Level 1) expand into detailed feature matrices (Level 2) before technical checkout (Level 3).
    *   **Plain-Language Taxonomies**: Technical specifications translated into clear, human-centered benefits.
    *   **Accessible Focus States**: High-contrast, non-blocking DOM elements (`aria-disabled="true"` for disabled states, `:focus-visible` outline rings).
*   **Pattern Transfer Rule for AI**: Map complex backend PMS database codes (`BAR-BB-NONREF-3N`) into clean, human-centered selection cards (*Flexible Rate* vs. *Stay & Breakfast*).

## 4. PATTERN TRANSFER AUDIT RUBRIC FOR AI AGENTS
When evaluating or generating a luxury hospitality interface, the AI agent must apply the following brand transfer verification checklist:

*   [ ] **Aman Test**: Is the viewport free of intrusive pop-ups, exit-intent modals, and flashing banners? Is negative space used intentionally?
*   [ ] **Belmond Test**: Is storytelling interleaved cleanly with booking entry points without feeling like a mass-market e-commerce grid?
*   [ ] **Rosewood Test**: Are accommodation options presented with spatial floor plans, exact square footage, and a 30/70 photography balance?
*   [ ] **Cheval Blanc Test**: Does the interface offer post-booking pre-arrival customization for dining, spa, and transfer preferences?
*   [ ] **RIMOWA Test**: Is the total stay price fully transparent up front without hidden drip pricing or artificial urgency tactics?
*   [ ] **Apple Test**: Are internal PMS rate strings translated into plain-language selection cards using a 3-tier progressive disclosure model?

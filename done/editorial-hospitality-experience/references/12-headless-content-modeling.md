## Reference Module 12: Headless Content Schemas (`headless-content-modeling`)

### 1. The 15 Core Hospitality CMS Content Schemas
To support omnichannel content delivery across marketing sites, mobile apps, in-room TVs, and guest portals, the API-first Headless CMS (such as CrafterCMS) defines 15 interconnected content schemas:

1.  **Property**: Global metadata, brand tier, location coordinates, contact gateways, direct booking perks.
2.  **Room Category**: Physical specs, square footage/meters, bed setup, max occupancy, view orientation, floor plan assets.
3.  **Room Group / Collection**: Groupings for suites, villas, or connecting room configurations.
4.  **Restaurant & Dining Outlet**: Culinary philosophy, chef profiles, dress codes, opening hours, reservation API hooks.
5.  **Bar & Lounge**: Cocktail menus, atmosphere media, seating capacities, entertainment schedules.
6.  **Spa & Wellness Sanctuary**: Treatment menus, practitioner specs, hydrotherapy assets, booking links.
7.  **Experiential Package / Offer**: Curated inclusions, validity dates, plain-language terms, pricing rules.
8.  **Event & Meeting Space**: Floor plans, capacity matrices, AV specs, banquet menus.
9.  **Destination Guide / Neighborhood Story**: Local cultural maps, curated itineraries, insider recommendations.
10. **Editorial Article / Magazine Spread**: Author bylines, reading duration, embedded room CTAs, visual spreads.
11. **Policy & Terms**: Plain-language cancellation schedules, deposit rules, pet policies, check-in windows.
12. **Amenity Spec**: Icon asset, spatial category, accessibility tags, plain-language description.
13. **FAQ & Concierge Knowledge**: Categorized guest Q&A, pre-arrival preparation, transfer options.
14. **Direct Booking Perk**: Rate parity terms, upgrade priority rules, complimentary inclusion tags.
15. **In-Stay Guest Service**: Room service menus, housekeeping requests, keyless entry preferences.

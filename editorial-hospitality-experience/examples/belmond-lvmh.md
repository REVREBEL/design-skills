### Brand 2: Belmond / LVMH (High-Editorial Magazine Storytelling)
*   **Core Philosophy**: Cinematic narrative spreads, historical romance, and magazine-grade art direction.
*   **UI Execution Patterns**:
    *   **Asymmetric Editorial Spreads**: 12-column layout grids pairing 16:9 cinematic imagery with offset typographic blocks (`measure <= 80ch`).
    *   **Contextual Booking Interleaving**: Booking CTAs are embedded directly into cultural and architectural narratives rather than isolated in aggressive e-commerce sidebars.
    *   **Editorial Micro-Copy**: Descriptive category titles ("The Venice Simplon-Orient-Express Journey") replacing transactional catalog headers.
*   **Pattern Transfer Rule for AI**: Interleave brand storytelling modules directly with direct booking entry points. Every three editorial story modules must be followed by a contextual suite category card.

# Editorial art direction as a decision system

## Determine the source of distinctiveness
Inspect the property's built architecture, geography, service culture, existing identity, photography, content, and intended guest. Extract 3–5 concrete brand traits, each supported by a visible or documented cue. Do not apply an assumed palette, serif, or monochromatic template simply because the segment is premium.

## Composition
Create a compositional grammar: content width, editorial margins, column counts, intentional exceptions, alignment anchors, caption placement, text-to-image relationship, and vertical rhythm. Different page purposes justify different densities. Maintain clear structural hierarchy and predictable mobile reading order even when desktop layouts are asymmetric.

## Typography
Define display, editorial body, utility labels, numerals, and forms by role, not merely by font name. Specify fluid scales, comfortable measures, typography contrast, line heights, weight selection, locale support, and accessible rendering. Evaluate real content: prices, dates, names, long translations, rate policies, and empty states.

## Photography
Separate immersive imagery from spatial evidence. Sequence room photography around guest questions and truthful property differentiation; use crop direction, focal points, aspect ratios, caption context, and fallback imagery. Do not invent missing floor plans or spatial attributes. The balance is contextual, never a mandated numerical ratio.

## Movement
Define motion vocabulary by function and emotional tone: reveal, feedback, transition, and orientation. Keep interaction response immediate; support reduced motion and no-animation transitions. Do not use cinematic effects to conceal a slow application.

## Systems with authored variation
Define tokens for foundation, then controlled composition variants for storytelling. Reuse functional controls consistently. Variation may occur in layouts, imagery sequence, and editorial pacing but not in the semantics of dates, rates, booking rules, forms, and navigation.

## Review lenses
Evaluate brand specificity, compositional hierarchy, visual pacing, typographic resilience, accessibility, room comprehension, conversion clarity, mobile adaptation, and maintenance cost. Identify one concrete rationale and one failure mode for each major design decision.

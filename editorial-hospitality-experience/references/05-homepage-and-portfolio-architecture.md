# Homepage & Portfolio Architecture (`05-homepage-and-portfolio-architecture`)

## 1. The 5-Stage Editorial Homepage Blueprint
Multi-property luxury brands require a homepage structure that alternates between magazine storytelling and commercial booking pathways:
1. **Stage 1: Editorial Hero View & Minimalist Search Capsule**: Full-bleed visual storytelling paired with a persistent, single-line search capsule (`Destination / Dates / Guests`) expanding via progressive disclosure.
2. **Stage 2: Portfolio Architecture & Experiential Grouping**: Categorizes properties geographically (e.g., European Retreats, Urban Flagships) or experientially (Wellness, Gastronomy, Beach).
3. **Stage 3: Editorial Cadence Spread**: Interleaves commercial property cards with magazine-style editorial features, embedding contextual CTAs leading directly to suite views.
4. **Stage 4: Direct Booking Value & Reassurance Bar**: Quietly details rate parity guarantees, flexible terms, direct perks, and member benefits.
5. **Stage 5: Global Access Footer & Concierge Gateway**: Houses currency/language selectors, member account access, direct contact channels, and pre-arrival portals.

*Adaptability Note: Single-property boutique hotels or small inn collections can streamline this blueprint into a unified 3-stage layout (Hero & Search -> Suite Showcase -> Reassurance & Concierge).*

## Reference Module 5: Homepage Cadence (`homepage-cadence`)

### 1. The 5-Stage Multi-Property Homepage Blueprint
To maintain brand voice across a multi-property brand without causing cognitive overload or structural clutter, the homepage layout follows a sequential five-stage editorial architecture:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ MULTI-PROPERTY HOMEPAGE ARCHITECTURE BLUEPRINT                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 1: Editorial Hero View & Minimalist Search Capsule                │
│   [ Full-Bleed Spatial Media / Floating Capsule: Destination/Dates/Guests ]│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 2: Portfolio Architecture & Experiential Discovery               │
│   [ Dual Taxonomies: Geographic Clusters vs. Lifestyle Themes ]        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 3: Editorial Cadence Spread & Brand Narrative                    │
│   [ Interleaved Magazine Spreads, Local Culture & Suite CTAs ]          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 4: Direct Booking Value & Reassurance Bar                        │
│   [ Transparent Rate Parity, Flexible Terms, Member Perks ]            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 5: Global Access Footer & In-Stay Concierge Gateway              │
│   [ Member Ecosystem, Language/Currency Selectors, Pre-Arrival Portal ]│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2. Stage Breakdown & Mechanics
1.  **Stage 1 (Editorial Hero & Floating Capsule)**: Full-bleed spatial imagery or ambient visual media establishing immediate visual atmosphere, paired with a floating, single-line search capsule (`Destination` / `Dates` / `Guests`). Interacting with this capsule expands an interactive slide-over drawer via progressive disclosure.
2.  **Stage 2 (Portfolio Architecture)**: Dual-axis property categorization allowing guests to explore by geographic region (e.g., European Capitals, Alpine Retreats) or experiential cluster (Coastal Sanctuaries, Gastronomy, Wellness, Historic Estates).
3.  **Stage 3 (Editorial Cadence Spread)**: Magazine-style content interleaving that breaks up commercial property cards with deep brand narrative spreads detailing culinary partnerships, architectural philosophy, and local neighborhood guides, embedded with contextual CTAs leading directly to specific room categories.
4.  **Stage 4 (Direct Booking Reassurance Bar)**: A restrained, dedicated view framing direct booking benefits: guaranteed rate parity, flexible cancellation terms, priority room upgrades, and direct concierge service access.
5.  **Stage 5 (Global Access Footer & Concierge Gateway)**: Clean navigation utilities housing localized currency/language toggles, loyalty account access, direct property contact channels, and pre-arrival concierge portals.

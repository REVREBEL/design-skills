## Preserved benchmark analysis

### Brand 5: RIMOWA / DTC Luxury Benchmark (Product Truth & Sludge Elimination)
*   **Core Philosophy**: Physical craftsmanship translated into digital engineering, radical transparency, and zero dark patterns.
*   **UI Execution Patterns**:
    *   **Transparent Total Pricing**: All prices displayed up front inclusive of all mandatory taxes, fees, and charges.
    *   **Material Specification Cards**: Detailed breakdowns of materials, dimensions, and durability specs.
    *   **Express 1-Tap Checkout**: Digital wallet integration (`Apple Pay`, `Google Pay`) streamlining checkout without forced account registration.
*   **Pattern Transfer Rule for AI**: Eliminate all mass-market urgency tactics (countdown clocks, artificial scarcity badges). Build conversion throughput strictly through direct value framing and transparent terms.

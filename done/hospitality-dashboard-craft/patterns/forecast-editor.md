# Pattern: forecast editor and approval
Show scope, period, baseline, actualized vs OTB vs forecast, last saved timestamp and author. Changes live in draft buffer until authoritative submit; highlight modified cells and effect on derived totals. Support keyboard cell movement without trapping focus, clear validation errors, undo within draft, save/discard/submit for approval and conflict resolution on revision mismatch. Approval cannot be inferred from optimistic UI. Missing/source-stale metrics may block particular actions according to verified risk policy, not a universal SLA. Mobile may supply approval/exception summary rather than full editable grid.

# Visual Aesthetics as Interaction Controls (`02-visual-aesthetics-and-mechanics`)

## 1. Functional Mechanics of Aesthetic Elements
In a high-function luxury editorial product, aesthetic elements operate as structural interface mechanics rather than passive decorative embellishments.

### Typography as Navigational Structure
Dramatic scale contrast replaces heavy structural borders, container boxes, and artificial dividers. Pairing high-impact editorial display headers with crisp, highly legible sans-serif body copy instantly establishes a clear visual hierarchy.
- **Reading Ergonomics**: Body text measure must be capped at 80 characters or fewer per line (`measure <= 80ch`) to preserve optimal reading comfort across viewports.
- **Operational Separation**: Enforce a strict functional separation between narrative display typography (used for mood setting and storytelling) and operational typography (nightly rates, availability badges, calendar grids). Operational copy employs tokenized spatial grids, tabular font alignments (`tabular-nums`), and high-contrast styling.

### Photography & Spatial Comprehension
Visual assets act as active portal entry points rather than background decoration:
- **Detail Crops (~30% starting benchmark)**: Evocative, tightly framed textural details (architectural materials, ambient light, room joinery) build emotional connection.
- **Spatial Views (~70% starting benchmark)**: Expansive architectural photography and window sightlines establish spatial orientation and booking confidence.

## Reference Module 2: Visuals as Structural Interaction Controls (`visuals-as-mechanics`)

### 1. Visual Aesthetics as Interaction Architecture
In luxury digital products, aesthetic choices—typography, photography, and spatial layouts—do not serve as decorative embellishments. Instead, they operate as structural, functional interface elements that guide guest cognition, establish wayfinding, and simplify complex choice evaluation without relying on heavy visual chrome, drop shadows, or artificial container boxes.

### 2. Gestalt Grouping & One Dominant Decision
To limit cognitive load, every viewport must communicate a single dominant decision point. Utilizing Gestalt principles of proximity and common region, related transactional elements—such as a nightly room rate, specific amenity icons, policy toggles, and the primary call-to-action (CTA)—are visually grouped into unified structural clusters.

## Workflow 3: Information Architecture & Guest Journey Mapping (`information-architecture`)

### Objective
Structure non-linear, unhurried guest journeys that guide users seamlessly from inspirational visual exploration through spatial verification to confident transactional commitment.

### Step-by-Step Execution

1. **Implement the 5-Stage Multi-Property Homepage Cadence**
   - **Stage 1 (Editorial Hero & Search Capsule)**: Full-bleed spatial imagery/media paired with a persistent, single-line floating search capsule (`Destination` / `Dates` / `Guests`).
   - **Stage 2 (Portfolio & Experiential Grouping)**: Categorize properties by geographical clusters or experiential lifestyle themes (e.g., Gastronomy, Alpine Retreats, Coastal Sanctuaries).
   - **Stage 3 (Editorial Cadence Spread)**: Magazine-style visual spreads interleaving cultural narratives with contextual room/suite CTAs.
   - **Stage 4 (Direct Booking Reassurance Bar)**: Quiet, high-contrast bar presenting rate parity guarantees, flexible terms, and direct perks.
   - **Stage 5 (Global Access Footer)**: Clean footer providing currency/language selectors, member account access, and pre-arrival concierge gateways.

2. **Establish 3-Tier Progressive Room Discovery**
   - **Level 1 (Story Card)**: Emotive imagery, category name, square footage/meters, bed setup, starting rate, and direct CTA.
   - **Level 2 (Spatial Overview Panel)**: Slide-over drawer disclosing vector floor plans, natural light orientation, bathroom specs, material details, and amenity lists.
   - **Level 3 (Transactional PDP)**: Real-time availability calendar, transparent rate plan selection cards, and pre-arrival customization toggles.

3. **Define Navigation & Persistent UI Controls**
   - Design sticky, non-intrusive header with minimal brand logo, primary navigation drawer trigger, and persistent booking trigger pill.

### Output Artifact
`information-architecture-blueprint.md` detailing site navigation trees, page template wireframes, and progressive disclosure user flows.

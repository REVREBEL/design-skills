# Brand Benchmark: Rosewood Hotels & Resorts
Residential luxury, spatial media balance, vector floor plan overlays, curated destination guides.


## Preserved benchmark analysis

### Brand 3: Rosewood Hotels & Resorts ("A Sense of Place" Residential Luxury)
*   **Core Philosophy**: Localized architectural context, residential warmth, and material craftsmanship.
*   **UI Execution Patterns**:
    *   **30/70 Spatial Photography Ratio**: 30% detail crops highlighting joinery, marble, and ambient lighting paired with 70% wide architectural orientation shots and vector floor plan overlays.
    *   **Localized Destination Guides**: Curated city and resort guides integrated directly into property navigation, positioning the property as a cultural gateway.
    *   **Spatial Room Framing**: Suite listings formatted like architectural residence showcases rather than standard hotel room inventory.
*   **Pattern Transfer Rule for AI**: Frame accommodations as physical, sensory living spaces. Require architectural floor plans, natural light orientation, and exact square footage/meters on every room detail view.

# Competitive UX & Choice Architecture Audit (`workflows/competitive-ux-audit`)
## Objective
Evaluate existing or competitor booking engines for choice overload, rate code sludge, dark patterns, and spatial opacity.

## Execution Steps
1. **Option Count Audit**: Check if room category lists exceed 5-7 initial options without category grouping or recommendation anchors.
2. **Rate Sludge Inspection**: Scan for raw database rate codes (`BAR-BB-NONREF`) presented without human-centered copy.
3. **Dark Pattern Check**: Identify artificial urgency badges, countdown clocks, or interruptive pop-ups to be eliminated.
4. **Spatial Spec Audit**: Verify if floor plans, exact square meters/footage, and view vectors are present.

## Workflow 2: Competitive UX & Choice Overload Audit (`competitive-ux-audit`)

### Objective
Evaluate existing digital properties and key luxury competitors against empirical choice architecture benchmarks (Chernev meta-analyses, Baymard usability research) to identify friction points, choice sludge, and dark patterns.

### Step-by-Step Execution

1. **Assortment Size & Attribute Alignability Audit**
   - Count total room categories surfaced during search. (Flag choice overload if > 6 room types are displayed simultaneously without clear categorization).
   - Assess attribute alignability: Can guests easily compare room square footage, bed setups, view orientations, and rate inclusions side-by-side?

2. **Choice Sludge & Jargon Identification**
   - Audit rate selection screens for internal database codes (e.g., `BAR-BB-NONREF-3N`, `PKG-SPA-2026`).
   - Identify redundant form fields, forced registration gates, or confusing cancellation policy terminology.

3. **Dark Pattern & Mass-Market Urgency Scan**
   - Inspect interface for mass-market urgency tactics: countdown timers, artificial scarcity banners ("Only 1 room left!"), intrusive pop-ups, or drip base-rate pricing.
   - Mark any identified dark pattern for immediate removal.

4. **Luxury Benchmark Comparison**
   - Compare guest experience against industry benchmarks (Aman, Rosewood, Belmond, RIMOWA).
   - Evaluate spatial storytelling balance, typographic legibility, and mobile thumb-zone ergonomics.

### Output Artifact
`competitive-ux-audit-report.md` detailing identified choice friction points, dark patterns, rate sludge instances, and prioritized recommendations.

# Layout Wireframing & Progressive Disclosure (`workflows/layout-wireframing`)
## Objective
Wireframe desktop and mobile viewports with persistent search capsules, asymmetric editorial spreads, and slide-over drawers.

## Execution Steps
1. **Desktop Spreads**: Layout asymmetric 12-column grids pairing 16:9 imagery with offset body text (`measure <= 80ch`).
2. **Mobile Thumb-Zones**: Anchor floating search capsules and primary CTAs within lower viewport thumb reach.
3. **Drawer Mechanics**: Design slide-over preview sheets for floor plans and rate selection without forcing full page redirects.

## Workflow 5: Layout Wireframing & Progressive Disclosure (`layout-wireframing`)

### Objective
Produce responsive wireframe specifications across desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports that combine spacious editorial art direction with touch-friendly interaction ergonomics.

### Step-by-Step Execution

1. **Wireframe Header & Search Capsule**
   - Desktop: Single-line floating capsule at top or bottom viewport.
   - Mobile: Persistent bottom-anchored pill measuring `≥ 44×44 pt` touch target.
   - Interaction: Interacting triggers smooth slide-over search overlay without changing page context.

2. **Wireframe Room Discovery & Spatial Overview Drawer**
   - Grid Layout: Asymmetric 2-column or 3-column editorial cards with 30/70 image previews.
   - Slide-Over Drawer: 50% width on desktop, 100% full-screen drawer on mobile. Features interactive floor plan viewer, view vector orientation, and sticky booking bar.

3. **Wireframe Plain-Language Rate Cards**
   - Vertical stack of radio selection cards separating *Flexible Rate* from *Stay & Breakfast*.
   - Includes inline expandable policy toggles for full deposit and cancellation terms.

### Output Artifact
`layout-wireframes.md` with viewport layout specs, component placement rules, and responsive breakpoint behaviors.

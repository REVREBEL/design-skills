# Non-Linear Guest Journeys & Discovery (`06-guest-journeys-and-discovery`)

## 1. Mapping the Non-Linear Luxury Funnel
Prospective luxury guests transition fluidly across channels before committing:
- **Inspiration Phase ("Dreaming")**: External editorial press, social media, and brand storytelling spreads.
- **Spatial Verification Phase ("Evaluating")**: Direct site visits to inspect floor plans, view vectors, transparent total rates, and cancellation policies (18% of travelers visit direct sites specifically for verification).
- **Transactional Commitment Phase ("Booking")**: Single-line search capsules, plain-language rate cards, and express digital wallet checkout.
- **Pre-Arrival Concierge Phase ("In-Stay")**: Post-booking pre-arrival portals for customizing transfers, dining, and spa schedules.

## Reference Module 6: Non-Linear Guest Journey Mapping (`non-linear-evaluation`)

### 1. Multi-Stage Lifecycle Modeling
Digital travel evaluation is inherently non-linear. Prospective guests transition fluidly across search engines, social channels, press coverage, and OTAs before landing on a direct hospitality flagship. The digital platform must support three continuous evaluation modes:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ THE NON-LINEAR LUXURY GUEST JOURNEY                                    │
│                                                                        │
│ [ Inspiration ("Dreaming") ] ──> [ Exploration & Discovery ]           │
│   • Atmospheric Spatial Media      • Experiential Portfolio Clusters   │
│   • Editorial Brand Stories        • Destination Culture Guides        │
│                                              │                         │
│                                              ▼                         │
│ [ Commitment ("Booking") ]   <── [ Spatial Verification ]              │
│   • Single-Line Search Capsule     • Floor Plans & View Orientation    │
│   • Progressive Slide-Over Sheet   • Detailed Architectural Specs      │
│   • Fast Digital Wallet Checkout   • PDP Level 2 & 3 Interactive Views │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2. The 5-Stage Connected Lifecycle
1.  **Marketing & Discovery**: Art-directed brand storytelling, spatial photography, and cultural guides.
2.  **Transactional Booking Engine**: Unified styling, smart defaults, transparent rate breakdowns, and express payment tokenization.
3.  **Pre-Arrival Portal**: Dedicated post-booking interface allowing confirmed guests to customize stay parameters—arrival times, pillow preferences, spa scheduling, and airport transfers.
4.  **On-Property Stay Experience**: Mobile keyless entry, smart in-room environment controls, and direct concierge messaging.
5.  **Post-Stay & Rebooking**: Frictionless digital folio review, express checkout, and private rebooking incentives that maintain a continuous, relationship-first connection.
